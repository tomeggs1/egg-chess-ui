The baseline matters here. Option 0 is what ships today: nav #2c251e against content that composites to #2c2822–#221f1a. That is a contrast ratio of 1.03–1.09, below the point where an edge is perceptible — so the question is less "how ornate" and more "there is currently no edge at all".

Options 1 and 2 reuse tokens the app already has (SURFACE_BORDER, CARVED). Option 3 reuses the dialogs' hairline idiom turned vertical. Options 4 and 5 introduce new ornament — judge them on whether they still look good after you have stared at a full-height strip of them, since the rail is sticky and always on screen.

Remember the rail collapses to a 72px icon strip. A 8px rope is 3% of the expanded width but 11% of the collapsed one.
